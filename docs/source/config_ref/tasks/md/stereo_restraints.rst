``stereo_restraints``
=====================

Hold protein stereochemistry through this md stage with NAMD extraBonds -- every alpha carbon that is L when the stage starts is held L (its N-C-CB-CA improper at -33 deg) and every trans peptide bond held trans. On by default for the first minimize after psfgen, where modeled residues are pushed hardest, and off elsewhere. A safety net, not a repair -- a center or bond already wrong is left alone -- and the stage reports afterwards, by residue, every one it had to hold against strain. Merged with ssrestraints when both are on.

Single-valued attributes:

  * ``enabled``: Hold L alpha carbons and trans peptide bonds for this md stage. Unset, it is on for the first minimize after psfgen and off elsewhere; true or false overrides that.

  * ``k``: Harmonic force constant of each held improper and omega (kcal/mol/rad^2) (default: 100.0)
