``stereo_restraints``
=====================

Hold protein stereochemistry through this md stage with NAMD extraBonds -- every alpha carbon that is L when the stage starts is held L (its N-C-CB-CA improper at -33 deg) and every trans peptide bond held trans. Off by default; a safety net for stages that move modeled residues hard (the first minimize after psfgen), not a repair, since a center or bond already wrong is left alone. Merged with ssrestraints when both are on.

Single-valued attributes:

  * ``enabled``: Hold L alpha carbons and trans peptide bonds for this md stage (default off) (default: False)

  * ``k``: Harmonic force constant of each held improper and omega (kcal/mol/rad^2) (default: 100.0)
